## Section (id="pricing"), heading: Pricing

**Company plan — $99 a month**
- Your company and 5 people. $12 a month for each extra person.
- Pay for the year and get 12 months for the price of 10 ($990 for the company and 5 people, $120 for each extra person).
- Everyone with a login counts as a person: pipelayers, foremen, superintendents, project managers. The company account is free and doesn't count.
- Everything in the app: grade and elevation calculators, runs with photos and sealed records, confined space permits, trench and shoring, daily reports, tickets, locates, the company board and office screens, crew invites, and Ask.

**Individual plan — $15 a month**
- One person, no company. Or pay for the year: $144, which is $12 a month.
- New Run, Quick Run, the calculators, Education & Reference, Stats, Jobs, Ask and stake reading.

Small print, same size as the rest:
- Prices are in US dollars, plus applicable tax.
- There is no free plan. Companies in the beta pay nothing until their beta ends; the price is shown in the app the whole time.
- In the beta now? Start your company plan before your beta ends and the price is locked for 12 months.
- Ask and photo reading have daily and monthly limits (a person on a company plan: 25 a day; a company shares 60 a month for each person on its plan; an individual plan: 15 a day, 50 a month). The app says when a limit is reached and when it resets.
- Plan reading is an add-on for company plans: $1 a sheet, charged once when the sheet is read. Questions about your plans after that count as normal Ask requests. [Decided Nov 20. Plan reading is not turned on for customers yet: keep this line, with "Coming soon" in front, until it is.]
