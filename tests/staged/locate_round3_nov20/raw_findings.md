# raw findings, round 3 (working file, all seen Nov 20 2026; quotes are the page reader's fragments, joined)
NV NAC 455.165 (leg.state.nv.us/nac/NAC-455.html): (1) valid "to conduct an excavation or demolition for 28 calendar days after the date and time that the excavator provided the notification"; (4) "...beyond the period afforded by the original notification and the subsequent extensions, the excavator shall submit a new notification to the association for operators" [pursuant to NRS 455.110]. Nothing in NAC 455 says a new notification ends or replaces the old one.
NV NRS 455.110(1)(a) (leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-455.html): "a person shall not begin an excavation or demolition" unless he notifies "at least 2 working days" and "not more than 28 calendar days before excavation or demolition is scheduled to commence." NRS 455.105: "“Working day” means every day from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m., except Saturday, Sunday and any federal or state holiday." NRS 455.130: operator locates "within 2 working days or within a time mutually agreed".
NV handbook 2025 p5: "You must have an active ticket for the entire duration of your excavation." "A ticket is active in Nevada for 28 calendar days from the date of issuance." p4: "at least two (2) working days (not including the date of notification) and up to 28 calendar days before you dig."; "the excavator may be liable for any damages or incidents that occur if excavation begins before the legal start date and time."; "Excavators are required by law to wait until all operators ... have provided a positive response before digging can begin, even if the start date and time shown on the ticket has passed." No sentence says a new ticket closes the old one. No sentence says "stop" at expiry in so many words.
AK handbook 2026 (cdn.pelicancorp.com/pdfs/26-ak811-excavator-handbook.pdf) p16: "Most facility owners consider a ticket "expired" if more than 15 calendar days have passed prior to excavation; or 20 calendar days have passed in remote locations." Remark: "be prepared to wait an additional 2 working days." "Please refer to the previously received dig number when calling for a remark." No fixed ticket life, no renewal rule.
AR Ark. Code 14-271-112 (findlaw, current as of Mar 28 2024): notice "at least two (2), but not more than ten (10), full working days before commencing"; "effective for a period of twenty (20) working days from the date that the notice was given."; not finished or marks gone: "reinitiate the notice procedure set forth in this section."
LA R.S. 40:1749.14(C)(1) (legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=169745): "the marking shall be deemed good as long as visible, but not longer than twenty calendar days, including weekends and holidays, from the mark-by time"; "An additional notice to the regional notification center shall be given by the excavator or demolisher when the marks are no longer visible or if the excavation or demolition cannot be completed" [within it]; forestry/agri/marine 30 calendar days; large project 90. R.S. 40:1749.13(B)(1): notice "at least forty-eight hours, but not more than one hundred twenty hours, excluding weekends and holidays, in advance"; (B)(5) "wait at least forty-eight hours, beginning at 7:00 a.m. on the next working day, following notification".
MO RSMo 319.026 (revisor.mo.gov, "Effective - 28 Aug 2025"): (1) "at least two working days, but not more than ten working days, before the expected date of commencing"; (7) "excavators may commence or continue to work within the area described in the notice until the notice of intent to excavate expiration date as long as the markings are visible." "If an excavator is unable to begin the excavation within ten working days as described in the request, the excavator shall make a relocate request before beginning the excavation." Expiration date not defined in this section.
