# Notice to crew about The Pipelayer's Bible (template for a customer company)

DRAFT for the lawyer. Not legal advice. A company would put this on its letterhead, hand it out at hire or before the
app is first used, and keep the signed copy. It does not replace the notice inside the app; it is the employer's own
notice. State add-ons are at the end.

---

**[Company name] uses The Pipelayer's Bible, a phone app, to record field work.**

**What the app records about your work**

- The runs, grade sheets, check shots and production you enter, and when each was made or changed.
- Photos you take in the app, stamped with the time, place and direction.
- Delays, breaks you mark with the Break button, and how long pipes took.
- Confined space permits: who entered, who attended, air readings typed in, and the times.
- Daily reports: the crew on site and the hours the foreman signs for each person.
- Toolbox talk attendance.

**What it does not do**

- It does not track where you go. Location is read only while a run screen is open (to stamp photos), when you ask
  for weather, or after the emergency button is pressed.
- It does not read your texts, calls, email, contacts or other apps.
- It is not the time clock. Your paid hours come from [the company's timekeeping system].

**Who sees it:** the office, managers and your foreman. People on your foreman's crew can see the crew's recent grade
sheets. The app's maker stores the records for the company.

**How we use it:** to run the job, bill the work, keep the records the law and our contracts require, and look at
production. We do not discipline or dismiss anyone on the app's numbers or its AI summaries alone; a person reviews
the records first.

**Your phone:** [The company provides a phone / pays $__ a month toward your phone because the app is required for
work.]

**Questions or a copy of your records:** ask [name, phone].

I have read this notice.

Name ____________________  Signature ____________________  Date __________
