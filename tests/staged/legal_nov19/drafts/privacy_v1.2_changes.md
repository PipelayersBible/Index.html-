## 5. Phone storage (replace the bullet)

Why: r17.08 stores photos on the phone separately and can offer to clear the ones the company already has.

> - **Phone storage.** The app keeps your work, including photos, on your phone so it works with no signal, and sends
>   it when the signal returns. Until a record has been sent, the only copy is on that phone. In Settings you can have
>   the app offer to clear photos from the phone once your company has them (after 30, 60 or 90 days, or never);
>   nothing is cleared without your tap, and the company's copy is not affected. Anyone who can unlock your phone may
>   be able to see what is on it. Use a screen lock. If you lose the phone, tell your company.

## 7. Security (replace the second paragraph)

Why: Civil Code §1798.82 as amended (SB 446, in force January 1, 2026): notice to affected California residents within
30 calendar days of discovery; a business that holds data it does not own notifies the owner "immediately following
discovery" (§1798.82(b)). Many crew accounts have no email.

> No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that information will never be lost or accessed without
> permission. If we learn of a breach of security that affects records in a company's account, we will tell the
> company's account owner without undue delay so the company can meet its own duties. Where the law requires us to
> notify you directly, we will do it within the time the law sets, by email if your account has one, and otherwise in
> the app and through your company.

## Not changed, checked

- **Children (section 9):** unchanged. The Service is a work tool for adults; nothing in r17.02–r17.10 is directed to
  children, so COPPA's "directed to children" / actual-knowledge triggers are not met.
- **Biometrics:** nothing collected is a biometric identifier. A drawn signature and a photo are expressly outside
  Illinois BIPA's definition (740 ILCS 14/10: "Biometric identifiers do not include writing samples, written
  signatures, photographs…"). No face or fingerprint matching exists in the app.
- **Location (section 5):** unchanged in substance. The crew notice in the app says location is used "only … to stamp
  photos", while this Policy also lists weather and the emergency button; align the notice text on its next version
  (see NOTES item 13).
- **Sentry:** already disclosed (no name, email or typed content; address not stored; can be turned off).
