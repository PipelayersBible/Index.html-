# Website wording (pipelayersbible.com): proposed changes

DRAFT. Source checked: `/tmp/claude-0/website` at commit 581f74b (Nov 19, 2026). Most of the site is already careful
(the FAQ and the confined space page carry the right limits). These are the lines that are stronger than the app or
the Terms.

| # | Page · current line | Why it is risky | Proposed line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home · Pipelayer: "Confined space permits and entry log, with a one-tap emergency call" | The button opens the dialer; a person still places the call (Terms 4.4; the site's own FAQ says so). "One-tap call" promises more | "Confined space permits and entry log, with an emergency button that opens the phone's dialer" |
| 2 | Home · Foreman: "Sees his crew's run progress live" | The app itself says "What crews' phones last sent … Not live if a phone is offline" | "Sees his crew's run progress as their phones send it" |
| 3 | Home · Office: "A sealed original of each run PDF, so any later change would show" | True only for that file, and only as a mismatch of its fingerprint (Terms 9.2) | "A sealed original of each run PDF, so a later change to that file would show" |
| 4 | Home · Office: "Search and export every record" | Absolute. Export exists for runs, daily reports, permits and spreadsheets; not shown for every record type | "Search your records and export runs, daily reports and permits" |
| 5 | Home · Office: "Built-in AI for civil work questions, crew management and your company's data" | "Crew management" by AI invites the reading that AI rates or manages workers (California SB 947, operative July 1, 2027; Civil Rights Council automated-decision rules) | "Built-in AI for civil work questions and for questions about your company's own numbers" |
| 6 | Home · Pipelayer: "Calculates pipe grades from survey stake photos or manually entered survey data" | The photo reading is AI and must be checked (Terms 5.2–5.3); the limit is only in the FAQ further down | "Works out pipe grades from survey stake photos (read by AI, then checked by you) or from numbers you enter" |
| 7 | Home · Foreman: "Warnings when an 811 ticket is nearly expired" | 13 states have no confirmed ticket life in the app's table; there the app gives no date | "Warnings when an 811 ticket is nearly expired, in states where the ticket life is set by law" |
| 8 | Home · trial steps: "Thomas sets up your company the same day" | A promise; elsewhere the site says "usually the same day" | "Thomas sets up your company, usually the same day" |
| 9 | Home · Office: "A PDF of every run, time-stamped at every point" | Times come from the phone's clock (Terms 5.6) | "A PDF of every run, with the phone's time at every point" |
| 10 | Footer / trial form | The Privacy Policy does not mention the trial form or GoatCounter (the footer does). No postal address anywhere | Add both to Privacy v1.2 (see privacy_v1.2_changes.md); add a postal address or registered mailbox to the footer and to marketing emails |

Not changed (checked, fine as written): "30 days free… No credit card, and no automatic payment" (matches billing
r17.02: a beta never converts by itself); "Does it replace our safety program? No…"; the trench tool page ("Your
competent person classifies the soil and decides the protective system. This page only does the arithmetic on OSHA's
table"); the confined space page ("It records what your crew enters…"); "Who owns our data? Your company."
