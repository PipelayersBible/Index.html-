On an iPhone, open the link in Safari. After you set your password:

  1. Tap •••, then Share. If you see Share already, just tap it.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Leave Open as Web App on.
  3. Open The Pipelayer's Bible from your Home Screen and log in there with your email and new password.

On Android, tap the menu ⋮ in Chrome, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

The app works without signal once it is on your Home Screen. On an iPhone, notifications only work from the Home Screen app.
