Free tools

Free tools for pipe crews

Calculators for the math that comes up in the trench. They run on the phone in the browser and nothing you type leaves it.

  • Pipe grade calculator

    Start invert, slope or drop, and length: invert elevations at every station, the fall and inches per foot. Plus the slope between two inverts.

    Open the calculator

  • Rod reading to invert

    Benchmark plus backsight is the HI. HI minus the rod reading is the elevation. Then the invert, with the rod on the invert, on top of the pipe or under it.

    Open the calculator

  • Trench sloping

    OSHA Table B-1 maximum allowable slopes for stable rock and Type A, B and C soil, and the top width of the cut.

    Open the calculator

  • Pipe volume and backfill

    Gallons of water in a run, and cubic yards of bedding and backfill for the trench with the pipe taken out.

    Open the calculator

The math on these pages comes from the same functions as The Pipelayer's Bible app: the same rounding, the same feet-inches to the nearest 1/16", and the same pipe tables. The app does a lot more with it: a whole run with hubs and fittings, the grade sheet, the run report, and the daily report.
