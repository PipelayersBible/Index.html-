For crews entering manholes and vaults

Confined space permit app

The entry permit and the entry log on the phone at the manhole, instead of a clipboard in the truck.

It records what your crew enters. The permit form doesn't replace your safety program, your air monitor, training or your competent person. The emergency button opens the phone's dialer to the number on the permit; someone still makes the call, and it needs signal.

The permit

The permit form has a place for each item OSHA lists for an entry permit, from the space and the purpose of the entry to the rescue services and how to summon them:

  • The space, its location and the jobsite
  • Purpose of the entry, and the date and authorized window (dates fill in on their own)
  • Entry supervisor by name, with initials authorizing the entry, and the attendant by name
  • Hazards of the space and the isolation and control measures
  • Acceptable entry conditions: oxygen, LEL, and optional CO and H2S limits
  • How you'll detect a ventilation failure, communication procedures and equipment provided
  • Rescue and emergency services, and the emergency number
  • Any other permits issued, such as hot work, plus notes and photos

Review it, then lock and activate it.

The entry log

Once the permit is active, the phone keeps the log while the work goes on:

  • Roster. Who's in the space and who's the attendant, with times in and out.
  • Air tests. Each reading is logged with the tester's name and the time. A reading outside the permit's acceptable entry conditions is flagged.
  • Reminders. Optional timers for the next air test and the next communication check.
  • Emergency. A button that opens the phone's dialer to the number on the permit, 911 unless you change it for a site with its own rescue.

When the job is done, the permit is closed and the record goes to the company. Closed permits and their air readings are kept.

No signal down the hole

Permits work with no signal. The record saves on the phone and sends when signal is back, and until it has sent, the only copy is on that phone. A phone call to 911 still needs signal.
