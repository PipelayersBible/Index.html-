Straight answers

Questions

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Runs, calculators and permits work with no signal. Records save on the phone and send when signal is back.

The AI features, signing in and anything shared with the office need signal, and until a record has sent, the only copy is on that phone.

What does it cost?

The company plan is $99 a month for your company and 5 people, and $12 a month for each extra person, plus applicable tax. Try it free for 30 days first: your company account plus 6 people, with no credit card, and nothing is charged automatically.

A pipelayer on his own can get the individual plan: $15 a month or $144 a year.

The calculators and guides on this site are free, with no account.

How do I get it on my phone?

It works on iPhone and Android and it isn't in the app store. Ask for a trial and Thomas sets up your company and emails an invite to whoever will run it. They open the link, choose a password, and add the crew from the app.

The app then shows how to put it on the home screen so it opens like any other app. On an iPhone: open it in Safari, tap ••• and then Share (or Share, if you see it), then Add to Home Screen. On Android: tap Install App in Chrome. Do this the first time where there's signal.

Does my crew need email addresses?

No. The office or a foreman can text a join link to a foreman or pipelayer. He taps it, agrees to the Terms and he's on the crew, with no email and no password. A link works once and lasts 24 hours.

Who is it for?

Crews that put sewer, storm drain and water pipe in the ground, and the people they report to. The pipelayer gets the grade sheet, check shots, the delay log and the permit. The foreman gets his crew's progress and a daily report that's already filled in. The superintendent, PM and office get every job, every signed report and the run records.

See grade and cut sheets, daily reports and confined space permits.

Who's behind it?

A pipelayer and underground foreman built it, and he's the one who answers: call or text (909) 435-5250 or email tfontaine@pipelayersbible.com.

Bigger than 6 people?

The free trial covers your company account plus 6 people. If more of your crew should try it, call or text Thomas at (909) 435-5250 about more seats. On a plan, every person past the first 5 is $12 a month.

Does it work in my state?

Yes. It works in every state. The 811 ticket dates, trench rules and serious-injury reporting are set by state, for all 50 states and DC. Where we haven't confirmed a state's own rule, the app says so.

What does the AI do and how much can I use it?

The AI reads survey stake photos, cut sheets and 811 tickets into the app, and answers civil work questions in Ask. You check what it reads before you use it. Each person on a company plan gets up to 25 AI requests a day (8 of them Ask questions) and 150 a month. The company account gets 5 a day. The individual plan gets 15 a day and 50 a month. During the free trial each person gets up to 30 a day. The app tells you when a limit is reached and when it resets.

Who owns our data?

Your company. Our Terms of Use say that, between your company and us, your company owns the records your crew puts in, and your account owner controls who can see them.

You can export your records from the app while the account is open, and for at least 60 days after it closes. We don't sell your information. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it replace our safety program?

No. The confined space permit form records what your crew enters. It doesn't replace your safety program, your air monitor, training or your competent person. The emergency button opens the phone's dialer to the number on the permit; someone still makes the call, and it needs signal.

Is it OSHA approved?

No, and nothing is: OSHA doesn't approve or certify apps. The permit form is built around the permit items in federal OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1206, and the trench calculator does the arithmetic on OSHA's Table B-1. Your state may be stricter than federal OSHA. Your competent person and your company's program decide what's safe and what's required.

Is the AI always right?

No. AI can make mistakes. It can misread a stake or give a wrong answer, so crews check every value against the stake, the plans and their own instrument before using it. On safety questions, your competent person and your site's program decide.

What happens after 30 days?

Nothing is charged automatically, and there's no card on file to charge. To keep it, the company plan is $99 a month for your company and 5 people, $12 a month for each extra person. Records already sent stay in your company's account and stay readable. If you don't continue, new runs wait on each phone until the company starts a plan.
