Grade and cut sheet app

Set up the run from the stakes or the cut sheet, and the phone works out the grade sheet: elevations and rod readings at every interval, with or without signal.

Start the run from what you have

Every run starts with survey data. In The Pipelayer's Bible you can get it in three ways:

  • Stake photos. Take pictures of the grade stakes and the app reads them and fills in the run.
  • The cut sheet. Import it as a PDF or a photo, or paste the rows. If the sheet has more than one pipe on it, you pick the run you're working today.
  • By hand. Type in the start, the end, the hubs and the slope.

The photo and cut sheet readers use AI, and AI can misread a number. Every value it fills in shows up for the crew to check against the stake, the plans and their own instrument before anyone lays pipe.

The grade sheet

Once the run is set up, the app builds the grade sheet: a row every interval you choose, such as every 25 ft, or at every joint when you give it the laying length. Each row shows the distance, the invert elevation and the rod readings for the invert and the bottom of pipe. It works with an engineer's rod in feet and hundredths, an architect's rod in feet and inches, or a Lenker rod.

The HI comes from a benchmark and backsight, or from a shot on a hub. Shoot more hubs and the app checks the HI against each one and warns you when they don't agree. Fittings and crossings sit on the sheet at their stations. As pipe goes in, laid rows are shaded, the next row is marked, and the footage shows at the top.

Check shots, slope and pipe info are all one tap away. If the inspector shows up late or you hit a utility, the delay log stamps the time, and that delay is already in the foreman's daily report.

When the run is done

End the run and the phone makes a PDF of it, with the phone's time at every point. The office gets a sealed original of each run PDF, so a later change to that file would show. The run goes to the foreman and the office when there's signal, and the footage counts toward the daily report.

No signal in the hole

Runs and calculators work with no signal. The run saves on the phone and sends when signal is back. Until it has sent, the only copy is on that phone.
