
## State rules addendum (Nov 21, 2026 — state audit)
Time sheets follow the job's state and city (then company state, then phone), with the strictest rule winning against federal law. Full findings: tests/staged/state_audit_nov21/timerecords/FINDINGS.md.
1. Dated per-state rules: daily overtime (CA >8 1.5x and >12 2x; AK; NV if paid under $18; CO >12; PR), 7th day (CA, KY), public-works daily overtime (OR, WA; check CA, NY, MA, NV), meals, rests, waivers, missed-break premiums (CA, PR, MN, CO, WA), show-up pay, split shifts, sick-leave accrual, city minimum wages.
2. Exact times, never rounded (CA: meals can't be rounded; Camp v. Home Depot still pending). Meals recorded as well as work periods.
3. Prefill stays as suggestions only: the foreman or worker confirms every hour, and the record shows which hours were typed and which came from runs/daily reports.
4. Worker e-signature each day (meals, rests, injury) and foreman approval, both with an edit trail; workers can see their own hours (CA: copies within 21 days).
5. Keep at least 6 years (NJ, NY, HI), kept after cancellation and exportable; public works 3 years after the job (IL 5).
6. Exports: WH-347 (2025 layout), CA DIR eCPR, WA L&I, IL IDOL portal (daily start/end, last-4 SSN), union fringe by member and classification. Full SSNs encrypted, office-only.
7. No biometric clock (IL, TX, WA, CO). GPS at the punch only, with a state crew notice first (CT, NY, DE, NJ, CA).
8. Until it ships: the daily report and run PDFs say they are job records, not time cards (staged patch), and "Crew hours for payroll" gets renamed.
