CA Gov 4216 (codes.findlaw.com/ca/government-code/gov-sect-4216/, "current as of January 01, 2026"): emergency "a sudden, unexpected occurrence, involving a clear and imminent danger, demanding immediate action"; tolerance zone "24 inches on each side of the field marking placed by the operator"; working day "a weekday Monday through Friday, from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., except for federal holidays and state holidays"; hand tool "uses human power and is not powered by any motor, engine, hydraulic, or pneumatic device"; legal start "two working days, not including the date of notification, unless the excavator specifies a later date and time"; delineate "to mark in white the location or path of the proposed excavation"; high priority: "high-pressure natural gas pipelines with normal operating pressures greater than 415kPA gauge (60psig)", petroleum, pressurized sewage, high-voltage 60kV+, hazmat pipelines; subsurface installation excludes nonpressurized sewerlines, storm drains, drain lines; area of continual excavation = agricultural operations and flood control facilities.

CA 4216.3 (findlaw): (c)(1)(A) "Every operator shall supply an electronic positive response through the regional notification center before the legal excavation start date and time."; (b) "If the field marks are no longer reasonably visible, an excavator shall renotify the regional notification center with a request for remarks." "the operator shall have two working days, not including the date of request, to remark the subsurface installation."; (a)(1)(C) abandoned: "indicate with an “A” inside a circle the presence of any abandoned subsurface installations, if known"; (d) excavator notifies center if operator fails to respond.
