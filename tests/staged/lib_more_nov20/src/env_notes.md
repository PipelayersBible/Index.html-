## EPA 2022 CGP as modified (April 2025) https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-04/2022-cgp-permit-as-modified.pdf
- 1.1.2 "Will disturb one or more acres of land" or part of common plan >= 1 acre. 1.1.3 site in area where EPA is permitting authority (Appendix B).
- 1.4.3 Table 1: NOI "At least 14 calendar days before commencing construction activities."
- expires "at 11:59pm, February 16, 2027."
- 2.2.3a remove sediment "before it has accumulated to one-half of the above-ground height of any perimeter control."
- 2.2.14 Table 2: initiate stabilization immediately when work permanently ceased or inactive 14+ days; complete "as soon as practicable, but no later than 14 calendar days" after initiating (5 acres or less disturbed at a time); 7 calendar days if >5 acres or sensitive waters.
- 2.3.4 washout: "leak-proof container or leak-proof and lined pit"
- 2.4.2 "Do not discharge visible floating solids or foam"; 2.4.3 no "visible sheen on the water surface"; 3.3.2 turbidity benchmark 50 NTU (dewatering to sensitive waters).
- 4.1 "A qualified person is someone who has completed the training required by Part 6.3."
- 4.2.1 "At least once every seven (7) calendar days" OR 4.2.2 every 14 calendar days + within 24 h of storm 0.25 in or more in 24 h (or snowmelt).
- 4.3 sensitive waters: every 7 days + within 24 h of storm. 4.3.2 dewatering: "once per day on which the discharge occurs."
- 4.4 reductions: stabilized areas (2x first month then monthly); arid/drought monthly; frozen suspend.
- 5.2.1 corrective action: new/replacement control or significant repair within 7 calendar days; otherwise by close of next business day. 5.2.2 dewatering: act immediately.
- 6.3 projects covered on/after Feb 17, 2023: inspector must "Have completed the EPA construction inspection course developed for this permit and have passed the exam," or hold current valid non-EPA cert.
- 7.3 SWPPP "at the site or at an easily accessible location". Records "for at least three (3) years". 8.4 NOT "within 30 calendar days after any one of the conditions in Part 8.2 occurs."

## EPA AWP for A/C pipe: 84 FR 26852 (June 10, 2019) https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2019-06-10/html/2019-12085.htm ; https://epa.gov/stationary-sources-air-pollution/notice-final-approval-alternative-work-practice-standard-asbestos
- approved "an alternative work practice, called close tolerance pipe slurrification (CTPS)" May 30, 2019.
- EPA "neither approved pipe bursting nor pipe reaming as AWPs to replace A/C pipe."; bursting/reaming over the RACM threshold must follow NESHAP incl. 61.154 active waste disposal site and 61.151 inactive site if left in ground.
- conventional: open trench, "wet-cut into 6- and 8-foot sections using a snap cutter or similar tool", wrapped; "release no visible emissions (VE) to the outside air"; "All ACWM must be kept adequately wet and sealed in leak-tight containers."

## OSHA letter of interpretation May 29, 2007 (AC water pipe tapping/tie-in) https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/2007-05-29
- competent person classifies; removal of AC pipe "is normally Class II asbestos work."; could be Class III if repair/maintenance and ACM fits one glove bag/waste bag; unclear -> "should assume the higher class applies."
- AWWA practices "are generally compliant with OSHA's Asbestos Standard for construction, 1926.1101."
- Class III training "shall take at least 16 hours"; Class II includes hands-on training. Wet methods, HEPA vacuums, prompt cleanup; no high-speed abrasive disc saws without controls, no compressed air, no dry cleanup.
- consultant "was in error when he stated that when an activity is below the PEL it is exempt from OSHA's Asbestos Standard."
