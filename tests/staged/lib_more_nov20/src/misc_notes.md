# Misc source notes (read 2026-11-20)
## AWWA C651-14 Disinfecting Water Mains (copy posted by Town of The Blue Mountains, ON) https://thebluemountains.ca/sites/default/files/2023-05/ANSI AWWA C651-14 Disinfecting Water Mains.pdf  (copyrighted: facts only, own words). Effective Feb 1, 2015; revision of C651-05. A C651-23 edition exists: NOT read.
- Tablet (4.3): 25 mg/L, hold >=24 h (48 h if water below 41 F); >=0.2 mg/L free chlorine detectable at end; fill velocity <=1 ft/s.
- Continuous feed (4.4): flush first at >=3.0 ft/s; 25 mg/L; 24 h; >=10 mg/L free chlorine at end.
- Slug (4.5): 100 mg/L, >=3 h contact; if below 50 mg/L stop and restore to 100.
- Spray (4.6): large mains that can be entered, 200 mg/L, 30 min.
- Bac-T (5.1.1): Option A: initial set, then second set >=16 h later, both pass. Option B: main sits >=16 h with no use, then two sets >=15 min apart, taps left running, no flushing. Every 1,200 ft + end of line + each branch longer than one pipe length. ~200 ft spacing if trench water/debris got in (5.1.1.3).
- Fail (5.1.6): reflush, resample; then rechlorinate (continuous feed or slug). HPC over 500 CFU/mL -> keep flushing (5.1.4).
- Repairs (4.11): controlled, not depressurized: swab/spray min 1% chlorine, flush 3 volumes until clear, no bac-T. Depressurized: 1% on exposed/accessible surfaces, flush 3 volumes, may return to service before results; one sample set downstream. Uncontrolled contamination: follow 4.3-4.6 where practical...
- Final connections (4.10): <= one pipe length, generally 20 ft or less: spray or swab 1% chlorine. Longer: set up above ground, disinfect, pass bac-T first.
## Fla. Admin. Code R. 62-555.340 https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-Ann-R-62-555-340 (amended 8-28-03)
- disinfect per "AWWA Standard C651, C652, or C653"; "a total of at least two samples - each taken on a separate day and taken at least six hours apart from the other sample(s)"; resample "until two consecutive samples at each sampling location show the absence of total coliform."
## 40 U.S.C. 3131, 3133 (Miller Act) https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title40/html/USCODE-2023-title40-subtitleII-partA-chap31-subchapIII.htm
- "Before any contract of more than $100,000 is awarded" performance + payment bond. (FAR raises working threshold to $150,000: existing chunk business-pm-21; not re-read.)
- "has not been paid in full within 90 days after the day on which the person did or performed the last of the labor" -> may sue on payment bond.
- "A person having a direct contractual relationship with a subcontractor but no contractual relationship" with the contractor: "on giving written notice to the contractor within 90 days"; "by any means that provides written, third-party verification of delivery to the contractor"
- suit "must be brought no later than one year after the day on which the last of the labor was performed"; waiver void unless in writing, signed, and "executed after the person whose right is waived has furnished labor or material"
