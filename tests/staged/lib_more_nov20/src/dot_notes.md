# DOT source notes (read 2026-11-20 via law.cornell.edu eCFR mirror; quotes are verbatim fragments)
## 49 CFR 395.1 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/395.1
- (b)(1) adverse driving: "may drive and be permitted or required to drive a commercial motor vehicle for not more than two additional hours"
- (e)(1): exempt from 395.8 and 395.11 if "The driver operates within a 150 air-mile radius (172.6 statute miles) of the normal work reporting location;" returns and released within 14 consecutive hours; 10 hours off between; carrier keeps time records 6 months.
- (e)(2) non-CDL property: exempt from 395.3(a)(2), 395.8, 395.11; 150 air-mile; "The driver returns to the normal work reporting location at the end of each duty tour;" no driving after 14th hour on 5 days of any 7, after 16th hour on 2 days; time records 6 months.
- (m): "any period of 7 or 8 consecutive days may end with the beginning of any off-duty period of 24 or more successive hours."
- (n): part does not apply to drivers of utility service vehicles as defined in 395.2.
## 49 CFR 395.2 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/395.2
- Transportation of construction material and equipment: "by a driver to or from an active construction site" "within a 75 air mile radius of the normal work reporting location of the driver"; State may set 50-75 for intrastate; excludes placarded hazmat.
- Utility service vehicle: CMV used to repair/maintain/operate utility facilities ... "operated primarily within the service area of a utility's subscribers or consumers"
- On-duty time: "means all time from the time a driver begins to work or is required to be in readiness to work" incl. inspecting/servicing CMV, driving, loading/unloading, "any other work for a motor carrier", compensated work for non-carrier.
- Adverse driving conditions: "means snow, ice, sleet, fog, or other adverse weather conditions" or "unusual road or traffic conditions" not known before dispatch.
## 49 CFR 395.3 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/395.3
- (a)(1) "A driver may not drive without first taking 10 consecutive hours off duty;"
- (a)(2) "A driver may not drive after a period of 14 consecutive hours after coming on-duty following 10 consecutive hours off-duty."
- (a)(3)(i) "A driver may drive a total of 11 hours during the period specified in paragraph (a)(2) of this section."
- (a)(3)(ii) "driving is not permitted if more than 8 hours of driving time have passed without at least a consecutive 30-minute interruption in driving status." (short-haul drivers excepted); break may be off-duty, sleeper, or on-duty not driving.
- (b)(1) 60 hours in 7 consecutive days (carrier not operating every day); (b)(2) 70 hours in 8 consecutive days.
- (c) 34 or more consecutive hours off restarts.
## 49 CFR 383.5 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/383.5
- CMV Group A: "having a gross combination weight rating or gross combination weight of 11,794 kilograms or more (26,001 pounds or more), whichever is greater, inclusive of a towed unit(s) with a gross vehicle weight rating or gross vehicle weight of more than 4,536 kilograms (10,000 pounds), whichever is greater"
- Group B: GVWR or GVW of 26,001 pounds or more. Group C: 16+ passengers incl. driver, or any size hauling hazmat as defined.
- GCWR: greater of manufacturer value on FMVSS label, or sum of GVWRs/GVWs of power unit and towed unit(s).
## 49 CFR 396.11 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/396.11
- report "at the completion of each day's work on each vehicle operated"; parts incl. "Service brakes including trailer brake connections", parking brake, steering, tires, "Emergency equipment".
- "Drivers are not required to prepare a report if no defect or deficiency is discovered by or reported to the driver."
- carrier must repair safety defects before dispatch and certify; keep "for three months from the date the written report was prepared."
- Not applicable to private motor carrier of passengers (nonbusiness), driveaway-towaway, or "a motor carrier operating only one commercial motor vehicle".
## 49 CFR 396.13 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/396.13
- "Before driving a motor vehicle, the driver shall:" "(a) Be satisfied that the motor vehicle is in safe operating condition;" (b) review last DVIR if required; (c) sign it.
## 49 CFR 390.5 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/390.5
- CMV: "any self-propelled or towed motor vehicle used on a highway in interstate commerce to transport passengers or property when the vehicle— (1) Has a gross vehicle weight rating or gross combination weight rating, or gross vehicle weight or gross combination weight, of 4,536 kg (10,001 pounds) or more, whichever is greater; or" ... (4) hazmat in a quantity requiring placarding.
## 49 CFR 391.45 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/391.45
- must be medically examined: not certified "during the preceding 24 months" (general); 12 months for insulin-treated diabetes, certain vision cases, exempt intracity zone.
## 49 CFR 396.17 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/396.17
- "Every commercial motor vehicle must be inspected as required by this section." "the tractor, semitrailer, and the full trailer (including the converter dolly if so equipped) must each be inspected."
- (c) not used unless each component "has passed an inspection in accordance with the terms of this section at least once during the preceding 12 months" and "documentation of such inspection is on the vehicle." (report or sticker/decal)
## 49 CFR 392.9 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/392.9
- cargo "properly distributed and adequately secured"; "tailgate, tailboard, doors, tarpaulins, spare tire and other equipment used in its operation" secured
- "Inspect the cargo and the devices used to secure the cargo within the first 50 miles after beginning a trip"; re-examine at change of duty status, "driven for 3 hours", or "driven for 150 miles, whichever occurs first."
