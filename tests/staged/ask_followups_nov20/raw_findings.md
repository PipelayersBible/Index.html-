## Washington trench (WAC 296-155 Part N) — current text at lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov
- 296-155-657(1)(a): "You must protect each employee in an excavation from cave-ins by an adequate protective system designed in accordance with subsections (2) or (3) of this section except when: (i) Excavations are made entirely in stable rock; or (ii) Excavations are less than 4 feet (1.22m) in depth and examination of the ground by a competent person provides no indication of a potential cave-in." History: WSR 16-09-085, eff. 5/20/16.
  https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/WAC/WAC%20296%20%20TITLE/WAC%20296%20-155%20%20CHAPTER/WAC%20296%20-155%20-657.htm
- 296-155-657(7)(b): dig "not greater than two feet (.61 m) below the bottom of a shield", only if designed for full depth and no sign of soil loss. (2)(d)(i)/(3)(d)(i): other designs "must be approved by a registered professional engineer."
- 296-155-66403 Table N-1 "MAXIMUM ALLOWABLE SLOPES (H:V) FOR EXCAVATION LESS THAN 20 FEET DEEP": Stable rock vertical (90°); Type A 3/4:1 (53°); Type B 1:1 (45°); Type C 1 1/2:1 (34°). Notes: (1) angles rounded; (2) "Sloping or benching for excavations greater than 20 feet deep must be designed by a registered professional engineer." NO short-term 1/2H:1V Type A footnote and no "short term exposure" definition (federal Appendix B Table B-1 note 2 has it). Only 1/2H:1V mention: (3)(c)(ii) cut back when signs of distress.
  https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/WAC/WAC%20296%20%20TITLE/WAC%20296%20-155%20%20CHAPTER/WAC%20296%20-155%20-66403.htm
- 296-155-655 (amended WSR 26-09-113, Order 21-20, filed 4/21/26, eff. 6/1/26): (3)(b) egress "in trench excavations that are four feet (1.22 m) or more in depth", "no more than 25 feet (7.62 m) of lateral travel"; (10)(b) spoil "at least two feet (.61 m) from the edge"; (11)(a) "Daily inspections of excavations, the adjacent areas, and protective systems must be made by a competent person" (+ before work, as needed in shift, after every rainstorm); (13)(a) "A competent person must remain on the job site any time trench excavation work is being performed."; (13)(b) "The employer must have a competent person complete a written trench excavation work plan" — not needed when "The trench is four feet or less and a protective system is not required" or trench sloped 1.5:1 or flatter; (13)(c)(xv) plan available on site; (13)(d) train employees on the plan before entry; (7)(a)(i) air test before entering excavations greater than four feet where a hazardous atmosphere may exist.
  https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/WAC/WAC%20296%20%20TITLE/WAC%20296%20-155%20%20CHAPTER/WAC%20296%20-155%20-655.htm ; https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/wsr/2026/09/26-09-113.htm

## State plans, trench (the 13 "unread" plans) — what each state's own page / rule says
- AK: 8 AAC 61.1010(c) adopts "29 C.F.R. 1926.10-1926.29, 1926.31-1926.1153, 1926.1200-1926.1213, and 1926.1400-1926.1442 as amended" (so Subpart P and Subpart AA), "except as provided in 8 AAC 61.1145 - 8 AAC 61.1170" (not read). osha.gov/stateplans/ak unique construction list: gases/vapors, steel erection and wood framing, electric power; no excavation. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/alaska/8-AAC-61.1010
- HI: HAR 12-110-50(a) incorporates 29 CFR Part 1926 "2017 Edition published as of July 1, 2017" with amendments in (b); none touch Subpart P or AA (only (b) was visible to the reader; (c)-(e) not seen). https://labor.hawaii.gov/hiosh/files/2018/12/12-110.pdf
- IN: osha.gov/stateplans/in: "IOSHA adopts all OSHA standards and regulations except it has a unique excavations standard." 620 IAC 1-1-30(7) adopts Subpart P as published 54 FR 45959-45991 (Oct 31 1989), no Indiana changes listed. IC 22-8-4-1: for a project "that may require the creation of a trench of at least five (5) feet in depth", "IOSHA regulations 29 C.F.R. 1926, Subpart P, for trench safety systems shall be incorporated into the contract" and the cost paid "as a separate pay item" or in the pay item of the principal work. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/620-IAC-1-1-30 ; https://codes.findlaw.com/in/title-22-labor-and-safety/in-code-sect-22-8-4-1/
- IA: 875 IAC 26.1 adopts 29 CFR 1926 by reference; no excavation or confined space exception; amendment list includes "80 Fed. Reg. 25518 (May 4, 2015)" (confined spaces in construction). https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/rule/12-11-2024.875.26.1.pdf
- KY: 803 KAR 2:415 Excavations: "the construction industry shall comply with 29 C.F.R. 1926, Subpart P, Excavations"; only Kentucky text = definitions of C.F.R., employee, employer. Eff. 8-3-2021. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/803/002/415/ (HB 398 of 2025 not checked.)
- MD: federal Subpart P by reference, with Maryland amendments to 1926.652(b)(3)(iii), (b)(4)(iii), (c)(2)(iii), (c)(3)(iii), (c)(4)(iii): keep the tabulated data / design / manufacturer's data at the jobsite until the protective system is no longer in use, then available to the Commissioner of Labor and Industry on request. No depth change. https://labor.maryland.gov/labor/mosh/reqprotect.shtml
- MN: MNOSHA fact sheet "Revised May 2026": "the trenching and excavation requirements of 29 CFR 1926.651 and 1926.652"; "Trenches five feet deep or greater require a protective system unless the excavation is made entirely in stable rock." Unique construction list on osha.gov/stateplans/mn has no excavation item. https://www.dli.mn.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/fact_trenching_excavation.pdf
- NC: state-specific standards page lists nothing on excavation or confined spaces (13 NCAC 07F .0201 itself not read). https://www.labor.nc.gov/north-carolina-state-specific-standards
- SC: Subarticle 7 is "identical to federal regulations contained in 29 CFR 1926" with listed changes; ONE excavation change: 1926.650(b) competent person definition adds "In order to be a competent person for the purpose of this standard one must have had specific training in, and be knowledgeable about soils analysis, the use of protective systems, and the requirements of this standard." https://regulations.justia.com/states/south-carolina/chapter-71/article-1/subarticle-7
- TN: TOSHA construction standards "are the same as the Federal Standard" except 1926.1-1926.4 not adopted. https://lwdsupport.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/115013202707-Differences-in-TOSHA-and-Federal-OSHA-Standards
- VT: "VOSHA has adopted OSHA standards by reference"; unique: PELs, lineworker safety. https://www.osha.gov/stateplans/vt
- VA: unique standards list (16VAC25-60, -70 telecom confined space, -73, -75, -97 reverse signal, -145, -160 sanitation, -180, -200, overhead high voltage) has no excavation and no construction confined space item. https://doli.virginia.gov/?p=23823054
- WY: "adopts all federal OSHA standards identically"; unique: oil and gas drilling / servicing, anchor standard. https://osha.gov/stateplans/wy
