FL 556.105 (m.flsenate.gov/statutes/556.105, 2026 statutes): (1)(c) "Information provided by an excavator is valid for 30 calendar days after the date such information is provided to the system." "In computing the period for which information furnished is valid, the date the notice is provided is not counted" "but the last day of the period shall be counted unless it is a Saturday, Sunday, or a legal holiday," [then] "the period runs until the end of the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or a legal holiday." (1)(a) "Not less than 2 full business days before beginning any excavation or demolition". No renewal timing in the section. sunshine811.com/faqs: "Flags and other marks are valid for 30 calendar days and may be removed after such time." nothing on renew lead.
IL 220 ILCS 50 (ilga.gov ActID=1286): Sec 2 "Extension": request "to extend the expiration date of a normal notice"; may be made "no earlier than the 20th day from the initial normal notice request" or latest extension request; extends "the expiration of the initial normal notice request or latest extension request by 25 days."; "may not be made simply to keep a prior notice open without continued excavation occurring." Normal notice: "at least 2 days, but no more than 10 days, before beginning the planned excavation or demolition"; "valid for 25 days from the date of the initial request". "Day" = 12:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m., not JULIE holidays, Saturdays, Sundays, or the day of the actual notice. 4(g): "the excavator shall pre-mark prior to requesting any subsequent extension notice."
NC G.S. 87-122(a) (ncleg.net Article 8A): "must be given no less than three full working days before the proposed commencement date"; "Notice given pursuant to this subsection shall expire 28 calendar days after the work start date." "No excavation or demolition may continue after this 28-day period" unless the person "provides a subsequent notice which shall be provided in the same manner as the original notice required by this subsection."
SC 58-36-60 (law.counselstack.com/statute/sc/36/58-36-60, version "effective until May 22, 2026"): (A) "within three to twelve full working days before the proposed commencement date"; (B) "Notice given pursuant to subsection (A) shall expire within fifteen working days after the date of notice." "No excavation or demolition may continue after this fifteen-day period unless the person responsible for the excavation or demolition provides a subsequent notice pursuant to subsection (A)."
