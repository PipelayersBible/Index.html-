### lib-ca-sp-10 [reask CA simple] How much water do I need on site per person?
   WHY: long: 76 > 70
   ANSWER (76 words): California requires at least 1 quart of fresh, cool drinking water per worker per hour for the whole shift (8 CCR 3395), which is 1 gallon per worker for a 4-hour stretch, or more for a full shift.

- Water must be free and close to the work, or you need a way to refill.
- Federal OSHA only requires an "adequate supply" and sets no quantity (29 CFR 1926.51).

### lib-us-osha-23 [reask KS simple] How wide is the top of a 10 ft deep trench sloped for Type B with a 3 ft bottom?

### lib-us-test-3 [fresh RI rule] How do they vacuum test a manhole?
   WHY: offer not wanted
   ANSWER (93 words): Plug and brace every pipe, seal a test head on top, pull 10 in Hg, then time the drop to 9 in Hg (ASTM C1244). [1][3]

1. Plug and brace all pipes with pneumatic plugs.
2. Seal the test head on a clean top.
3. Pull 10 in Hg, close the valve, shut off the pump.
4. Time 10 to 9 in Hg; it passes if the time meets the table for its size and depth.
5. If it fails, repair the leak and retest.
