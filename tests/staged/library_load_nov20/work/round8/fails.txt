### lib-wy-dig-3 [fresh WY steps] We just nicked a gas line with the bucket. What do we do?
   WHY: facts: operator | buried: 911 | no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (100 words): 1. Stop work and shut off the equipment if it is safe to do so.

### lib-us-osha-28 [fresh ND rule] How do I do the thumb test on soil?
   WHY: long: 106 > 100 | no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (113 words): Press your thumb into a fresh, undisturbed clump of spoil (like a large chunk from the bucket) as soon as it's dug, and note how far it goes in (OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, Appendix A).
1. Take a large, undisturbed clump of spoil right after excavation.
2. Push your thumb in. If it only dents with very great effort, that points to Type A.
3. If your thumb goes in several inches easily and light finger pressure molds it, that is Type C.
4. Redo it if rain or flooding wets the excavation, and reclassify.

- It is for cohesive soil only, not sand.
- Your competent person decides on site.
