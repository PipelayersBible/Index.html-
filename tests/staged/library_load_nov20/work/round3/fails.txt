### lib-us-osha-12 [reask OK steps] What does the competent person have to do on an excavation?
   WHY: facts: classif
   ANSWER (65 words): The competent person inspects the excavation, adjacent areas and protective systems daily before work starts, as needed during the shift, and after every rainstorm or other hazard-increasing event, and pulls workers out if a hazard shows up (29 CFR 1926.651(k)), which is the federal rule Oklahoma follows.

### lib-us-test-3 [reask RI rule] How do they vacuum test a manhole?
   WHY: offer not wanted
   ANSWER (97 words): 1. Plug every pipe into the manhole with pneumatic plugs and brace them (ASTM C1244).
2. Seat the test head on a clean, smooth surface at the top.
3. Pull a vacuum of 10 inches Hg, then close the valve and shut off the pump.
4. Time how long the vacuum takes to drop to 9 inches Hg.
5. It passes if the time meets or beats the table value for its diameter and depth.
6. If it fails, repair the joint, lift hole or boot and retest.

### lib-us-mutcd-6 [fresh KS simple] How long is a shoulder taper?
   WHY: long: 78 > 70
   ANSWER (78 words): A shoulder taper is at least 1/3 of L, where L = W x S x S / 60 at 40 mph or less and L = W x S at 45 mph or more (MUTCD 6B.08, Table 6B-3).
- Example: an 8 ft shoulder at 45 mph is 120 ft.
- Kansas edition of the MUTCD is not confirmed; ask KDOT's traffic office, and follow the approved traffic control plan.
- Your competent person decides on site.
