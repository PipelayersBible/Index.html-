### lib-us-osha-12 [redo OK steps] What does the competent person have to do on an excavation?
   WHY: facts: classif | no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (99 words): Under federal OSHA, which covers Oklahoma private crews, the competent person must inspect and fix hazards, using these steps (1926.651(k)):
1. Inspect the excavation, adjacent areas and protective systems before work starts.
2. Inspect again as needed through the shift and after every rainstorm or other hazard-increasing event.
3. Remove exposed workers if you find cave-in signs, protective system failure or a hazardous atmosphere.
4. Decide what protection the trench gets; a system is required at 5 ft or deeper (1926.652(a)(1)).
5. Monitor water removal equipment and inspect after heavy runoff (1926.651(h)).
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-mutcd-6 [redo KS simple] How long is a shoulder taper?
   WHY: long: 74 > 70 | offer not wanted
   ANSWER (92 words): A shoulder taper is at least one third of the merging taper length L (MUTCD Table 6B-3), using the shoulder width as W.
- L = W x S x S / 60 at 40 mph or less, and L = W x S at 45 mph or more; an 8 ft shoulder at 45 mph is 120 ft.
- Kansas uses the national MUTCD formulas; the approved traffic control plan or permit governs.
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-mutcd-56 [redo KS simple] When is a job long-term stationary?
   WHY: long: 74 > 70
   ANSWER (81 words): The MUTCD (Section 6N.01) calls traffic-control work long-term stationary when it stays in one spot more than 3 days. OSHA's trench rules (Subpart P) have no "long-term stationary" category.

- Intermediate-term is more than one daylight period up to 3 days, or night work over 1 hour.
- Long-term zones run into the night, so devices must be retroreflective or illuminated.
- Your state manual and the approved traffic control plan govern the job.
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-osha-33 [reask AL simple] Does the top trench box in a stack need to be rated for the whole depth?
   WHY: long: 74 > 70
   ANSWER (81 words): No, OSHA says stacked shields don't all have to be designed for the deepest part of the trench; each box only has to be used at a depth not greater than it is designed for (OSHA letter, Oct 29, 1991; 29 CFR 1926.652(c)(2)).
- Stack only as the manufacturer's tabulated data allows, with the deeper-rated box on the bottom.
- Keep the data sheet on site, and pin the boxes together as it directs.
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-mutcd-40 [reask OK rule] Where should the flagger stand?

### lib-us-fed-11 [reask ND complex] Does a utility service truck have to follow hours of service? We are putting in new water main for a developer.
   WHY: no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (76 words): Yes, if the truck is over 10,001 lb and crosses state lines, its driver is under federal hours of service (49 CFR 390.5, Part 395), unless an exception applies.
- The utility service vehicle exemption (395.1(n)) fits repair and maintenance work; new water main for a developer is not confirmed, so ask North Dakota DOT enforcement.
- If it mainly hauls materials or equipment to the site within 75 air miles, the 24-hour restart applies (395.1(m)).

### lib-us-fed-19 [reask CO complex] Can we pipe burst an old asbestos cement water main?
   WHY: no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (70 words): Not as a normal method: EPA has not approved pipe bursting for asbestos-cement water mains, so bursting more than the threshold amount has to follow the Asbestos NESHAP (84 FR 26852).
- EPA did approve one trenchless method, close tolerance pipe slurrification, with wetting, sampling and landfill disposal.
- Get the owner's and the air agency's position in writing before bidding or starting.
- Your competent person decides on site.
