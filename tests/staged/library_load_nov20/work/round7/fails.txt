### lib-us-fed-9 [reask MS rule] How many gas cans can we carry in the crew truck before we need hazmat placards?
   WHY: long: 220 > 100
   ANSWER (220 words): There is no limit on the number of cans; the limits are 8 gallons per can and 200 pounds (440 lb) gross weight for everything on the truck together. Stay inside both and you need no placards or shipping papers under the materials of trade rule, 49 CFR 173.6 [1].

- Each gasoline can: no more than "30 L (8 gallons)" [1].
- All materials of trade on the truck combined (fuel, oil, spray paint and the like): no more than "200 kg (440 pounds)" gross weight [1]. Gasoline weighs about 6 lb per gallon, so the weight limit usually decides it before the can count does. A full 5-gallon can with its container is roughly 35 lb or more, which puts you around 12 cans, and that is before anything else on the truck.
- Cans must be metal or plastic safety cans, closed, secured and marked "gasoline". The driver must be told the hazardous material is on board [1].
- Diesel in non-bulk containers can be treated as a combustible liquid, and the hazmat rules then don't apply. A large diesel transfer tank is bulk packaging with its own marking and placard rules, so check the tank size before you buy one [1].

This is the federal rule. A company policy or a state rule can be stricter.

### lib-us-osha-48 [style MO complex] What has to be on a confined space entry permit?
