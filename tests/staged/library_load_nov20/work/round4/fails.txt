### lib-us-osha-62 [reask PA simple] How strong does a cover over a hole in the road have to be?

### lib-mi-sp-1 [fresh MI rule] What depth needs sloping or shoring?
   WHY: facts: Part 9
   ANSWER (65 words): In Michigan, the side of an excavation more than 5 feet deep must be sloped per MIOSHA's table 1 or supported (R 408.40941(1)); a shallower one is protected too if the ground shows signs of hazardous movement.

- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-fed-17 [fresh NH simple] How long do we keep the SWPPP inspection reports after the job?
   WHY: long: 156 > 70
   ANSWER (156 words): Most likely 3 years. EPA's Construction General Permit (CGP) says to keep your SWPPP, inspection reports and other permit records for at least 3 years after permit coverage ends, which means after you file the Notice of Termination. New Hampshire is an EPA-authority state, so the EPA CGP generally applies there. I'm giving the 3 years from general knowledge of the permit text, not from an excerpt.

- The clock starts when coverage is terminated (the NOT), not when the last work is done. Closing the SWPPP/stormwater permit is on the closeout checklist [8].
- Your contract, an owner or agency requirement, or a state or local permit can require longer. If any of them does, follow the longer period.
- Inspection frequency and the rest of the permit details are in your permit [2]. Check the exact retention wording in your permit's record-keeping section.

The app's libraries don't cover this yet; this is general knowledge.

   WHY: long: 120 > 110

5. Rescue and emergency services and how to summon them.
6. Communication procedures, plus any other items on the full list.

### lib-us-fed-11 [style ND complex] Does a utility service truck have to follow hours of service? We are putting in new water main for a developer.

### lib-us-fed-19 [style CO complex] Can we pipe burst an old asbestos cement water main?
   WHY: no offer at the end (complex)
   ANSWER (64 words): Not as a standard method: EPA has not approved pipe bursting or reaming for asbestos-cement pipe, and the broken pipe left in the ground falls under the Asbestos NESHAP (84 FR 26852) [1].
- EPA did approve close tolerance pipe slurrification, a wet grind-out with asbestos-waste disposal [1].
- Get the owner's and the air agency's position in writing before bidding or starting [1].
