### lib-us-osha-12 [reask OK steps] What does the competent person have to do on an excavation?
   WHY: facts: classif
   ANSWER (115 words): OSHA requires the competent person to inspect the excavation, adjacent areas and protective systems daily, and pull workers out if they find a hazard (29 CFR 1926.651(k)).

1. Inspect before work starts and as needed through the shift.
2. Inspect again after every rainstorm or other hazard-increasing event.
3. Look for cave-in signs, protective system failure, hazardous atmospheres and other hazards.
4. Remove exposed workers until the hazard is fixed.
5. Monitor water-removal equipment if it is controlling water in the excavation.
6. Decide the protection needed, since the competent person has authority to stop work.

- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-osha-33 [reask AL simple] Does the top trench box in a stack need to be rated for the whole depth?
   WHY: long: 74 > 70
   ANSWER (81 words): No. OSHA says stacked boxes don't all have to be designed for the deepest part of the trench; each box only has to be used at a depth no greater than it is designed for (OSHA letter, Oct 29, 1991) [9].
- Stack only as the manufacturer's tabulated data allows, and put the deeper-rated box on the bottom.
- Keep the data sheet on site and pin the boxes as the data says (1926.652(c)(2)).
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-in-dig-2 [reask IN simple] How many days before we dig do I have to call 811?
   WHY: long: 79 > 70
   ANSWER (79 words): In Indiana, call at least two full working days before you dig, and no more than ten calendar days ahead (Ind. Code 8-1-26-16). [1]

### lib-us-mutcd-6 [reask KS simple] How long is a shoulder taper?
   WHY: long: 85 > 70
   ANSWER (92 words): A shoulder taper is at least one third of L (the merging taper length), using the shoulder width as W (MUTCD Part 6, Table 6B-3) [4].
- L = W x S x S / 60 at 40 mph or less, L = W x S at 45 mph or more; an 8 ft shoulder at 45 mph is 120 ft [4][7].
- In Kansas the national MUTCD sets tapers unless KDOT's manual or your permit says otherwise; the approved traffic control plan governs [1].
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-ca-sp-6 [fresh CA simple] How far apart are trench ladders here?
   WHY: long: 74 > 70
   ANSWER (81 words): California (8 CCR 1541(c)(2)) and federal OSHA both require a ladder, stairway, ramp or other safe way out in trenches 4 feet or deeper, placed so no one has more than 25 feet of lateral travel to reach it.

- That puts ladders about 50 ft apart at most; Greenbook 306-3.6 says every 50 ft in trenches over 4 ft deep.
- Ladder side rails must extend at least 3 ft above the landing.
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-osha-35 [fresh FL simple] Does the trench box have to stick up 18 inches above the ground?
   WHY: long: 73 > 70
   ANSWER (80 words): No, OSHA does not require a trench box to stick up above ground level; the 18 inches applies only when the box sits in the vertical lower part of a cut that has a sloped cut above it (29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, Appendix B).
- Florida follows federal OSHA for private crews, so the same answer applies.
- Spoil and materials still stay at least 2 ft back from the edge (1926.651(j)(2)).
- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-fed-9 [fresh MS rule] How many gas cans can we carry in the crew truck before we need hazmat placards?
   WHY: long: 163 > 100
   ANSWER (163 words): There is no set number of cans. The limits are 8 gallons per container and 440 pounds gross weight for everything hazardous on the truck combined. Stay inside both and you need no placards or shipping papers [1].

- Each gasoline can: no more than "30 L (8 gallons)" [1].
- All materials of trade on the truck together: no more than "200 kg (440 pounds)" gross weight, counting the cans, saw fuel, oils and any other hazmat [1].
- Cans must be metal or plastic safety cans, closed, secured and marked "gasoline", and the driver must be told what is on board [1].
- Diesel in a non-bulk container can be treated as a combustible liquid and is not covered by the hazmat rules [1]. A big diesel transfer tank is bulk packaging with its own marking and placard rules, so check before buying one.

### lib-us-std-more-3 [fresh NV rule] Do I have to chlorinate and sample the tie-in piece? Its one 18 ft stick.
   WHY: no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (78 words): Under AWWA C651-14 you chlorinate it but don't sample it: an 18 ft stick is under one pipe length (generally 20 ft or less), so swabbing or spraying the inside with at least 1 percent chlorine just before it goes in is the rule (Sec. 4.10) [1].
- A connection longer than that must be disinfected and pass bac-T samples before install [1].
- The spec, water agency and state health rules govern; they can ask for more.
