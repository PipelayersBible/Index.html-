### lib-us-osha-48 [style MO complex] What has to be on a confined space entry permit?
   WHY: long: 169 > 110 | no offer at the end (complicated topic)
   ANSWER (176 words): OSHA requires the entry permit to identify the space, the purpose, the date and duration, the people in each role, the hazards and controls, the acceptable entry conditions, test results, rescue, communication and equipment (29 CFR 1926.1206).

1. The space, the purpose of entry, and the date and authorized duration.
2. Entrants (by name, roster or tracking system), attendants by name, and the entry supervisor by name with signature or initials.
3. The hazards of the space, and the isolation and control measures used before entry.
4. The acceptable entry conditions, and test results with the tester's name or initials and when tested.
5. Rescue and emergency services and how to summon them, plus communication procedures.
6. Equipment (PPE, testing, communications, alarms, rescue), other safety information, and other permits such as hot work.

The permit also has to say how a rise in air hazard is detected if ventilation stops. The supervisor signs before entry, and the entry employer keeps each canceled permit for at least 1 year (1926.1205).

- Your competent person decides on site.

### lib-us-mutcd-54 [style NE complex] We have to close the sidewalk for the trench. What do we have to do for people walking?
   WHY: long: 131 > 110
   ANSWER (151 words): 1. Give people a safe, accessible detour or temporary walkway, and send them across at an intersection, not mid-block (6C.02).
2. Post SIDEWALK CLOSED (R9-9) at the closure, and SIDEWALK CLOSED AHEAD CROSS HERE (R9-11) at the crossing before it.
3. Span the full width of the closed sidewalk with a continuous barrier a long cane can detect; tape, rope and cones alone don't count (6C.03).

5. Make temporary ramps 12:1 or flatter and slip-resistant, with a 48 by 48 in. landing.
6. Cover or fence the open trench next to the walkway, and use temporary barrier where the route runs close to traffic.

   ANSWER (74 words): Not as a normal method: EPA has not approved pipe bursting or reaming for asbestos-cement (AC) pipe, so it falls under the Asbestos NESHAP waste rules (40 CFR 61.154 and 61.151) (84 FR 26852).

### lib-la-dig-3 [fresh LA rule] What's the fine for digging without calling 811?
   WHY: long: 118 > 100
   ANSWER (118 words): I don't have a confirmed fine amount for digging in Louisiana without calling 811, so ask Louisiana 811 or the state agency that enforces the dig law.

- Louisiana's dig law is the Underground Utilities and Facilities Damage Prevention Law (R.S. 40:1749.11 to 1749.27), and it requires notice 48 to 120 hours ahead, not counting weekends and holidays.
- Hitting a pipeline with no ticket can also bring a federal enforcement case from PHMSA, with civil penalties and possible criminal charges (49 CFR 196). The dollar amounts change each year.
- Whatever the fine, you pay for the damage and any injuries, and a call before you dig protects you from civil liability if you follow the law.
